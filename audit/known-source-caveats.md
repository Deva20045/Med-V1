This companion follows the scanned *PULSE Medicine Vol 1*, Marrow Edition 8, page by page. Questions that reproduce a source statement are labelled as such; they are not independent clinical recommendations. The standalone app carries a matching study-only disclaimer.

## General use note

All chapters are educational study material keyed to a particular scanned textbook edition. Treatment recommendations, thresholds and diagnostic labels are reproduced for source coverage and may be incomplete, outdated, ambiguous or erroneous. They are not a substitute for current clinical guidelines, prescribing references, or patient-specific medical care.
